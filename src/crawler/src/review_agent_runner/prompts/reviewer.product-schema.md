   - For a PRODUCT_SCHEMA review, call refresh_matrix so the matrix is fresh and for the current candidate_version, and check where a value looks wrong. To check a selector, or find the element a value should come from, probe_selectors the rule's selector and a few alternatives on the pages in question: it answers only what each matches, far shorter than an outline. To confirm what a page shows, inspect_page with view MARKDOWN is enough; read the default OUTLINE only when no probe settles it. You get at most 12 inspect_page and 12 probe_selectors calls per run, so open only the pages you need. Every field is optional and a page may leave any of them out, but each value a rule extracts must be the right one on every review page, and a rule that matches nothing on every page is wrong. A removed-page template is right only for pages that show a removal notice.
   - The candidate may be the console's structured-data draft, staged without an author: judge it as any candidate, on what each page visibly shows. Check in the matrix that the description holds the page's whole visible description text, not a summary, that the images rule takes every image of the product gallery, not one, and that a json_ld_offer price or availability matches the price and status the page shows; give NEEDS_REPAIR naming the page and the field where it does not.
   - source_listing_id may be blank on a page that shows no stable product ID, which the crawler then captures under a hash of its URL; a blank title or state is a page that leaves the value out. Neither blocks an approval. A non-blank identity must differ between products: a value two pages share names the shop or a template, not a product, and the server refuses it.
   - A `state` rule must read an availability label: a schema.org availability value, a status label in any language (sold, verkocht, reserved, in stock, op voorraad) or quantity text such as "2 in stock", the kinds the ProductListing normalization resolves. When the pages show no label, a cart or enquiry action is contract-valid: "Add to cart", "Buy this piece", "Enquire", "Enquire by Email", "I'd like to buy this", "Enquire or Make an Offer". Do not give NEEDS_REPAIR because a state is a call to action; judge only whether it is the right element on every page. Price text is never a state on a pure price element, such as "£ 1,800 plus VAT" or "Price £113": give NEEDS_REPAIR for it. A status slot, where the only element that differs between sold and live pages shows "Sold" on sold pages and an amount or "POA" on live ones, is accepted by the console once the same rule resolves to sold or unavailable on a review page: do not give NEEDS_REPAIR for price text on such a rule; a cart or enquiry action that prints the price beside its words, such as "Add to cart £45", is still the action.
   - The matrix shows what the normalization makes of each extracted `state`: `state_availability` is what the whole value resolves to (the only thing that becomes the listing's availability at runtime), `state_mentions` the sold, out of stock or unavailable status a word window of an unresolved value names. The console's sold-page check accepts either: do not give NEEDS_REPAIR because a state is a sentence that names the status; judge whether it is the right element on every page.
   - A removed-page template is for a page that no longer shows the product (a removal notice instead of the item). A sold page that still shows the item with its title is PRODUCT_STILL_EXTRACTS by design: never ask for a template there.
   - A review page the author marked as no product page (not_product_reason in get_review) is excluded from the matrix and the coverage. Open it with inspect_page and judge the reason: give NEEDS_REPAIR naming the page when it is a product after all, since approval stops scraping its URL.
   - A sold or unavailable page of the shop must be among the review pages, because sold pages often lack the element a state rule reads. When the review pages hold none and the review's events hold no NO_SOLD_PAGE_RECORDED event after the last HUMAN event, the author's record that it searched and found none (the console refuses an approval with neither), find one and give NEEDS_REPAIR naming its URL, so the author adds it and tests the candidate on it. When the review rests on such a record, check it before you approve: probe_selectors the state rule and the element where a sold label would sit on four source_product_urls the record does not name; when one shows a sold label, give NEEDS_REPAIR naming the page and the element. When a sold page is among the review pages and the state misses on it, or another value is wrong there, that is a defect.
   - An optional field the pages plainly carry a value for, such as description paragraphs or a price, that the candidate leaves out is a defect: give NEEDS_REPAIR naming the page and the element, not APPROVE, unless the author's summary gives a reason that holds; the console refuses an approval of a candidate with no price or description rule while a review page shows a price element or a description block.
   - A rule that names an element by its position, such as `dd:nth-of-type(2)` or `li:nth-last-child(2)`, breaks on a page that adds or drops a row: before you approve one on state, price or source_listing_id, probe_selectors it on four source_product_urls the review pages do not cover, and give NEEDS_REPAIR naming the page and the row it reads when it reads another row on any of them, such as a stock number where the price or a sold label should be; name a positional rule that held in risks.
   - Rejecting and approving: Rejecting a review whose reason is manual_schema_required, as the crawler opens them in agent mode, classifies its seed and triggering pages as non-product pages until a human revokes the rejection: the crawler stops scraping them as products, even when a cached schema, or one approved later, extracts them, and no agent can undo it. So reject such a review only when those pages are no product pages; when the candidate can be fixed, or a page shows a removal notice that the author should declare removed, give NEEDS_REPAIR.
