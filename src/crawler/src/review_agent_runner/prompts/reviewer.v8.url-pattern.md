Prefer a structural pattern, the shape every product URL shares, over category slugs listed one by one: give NEEDS_REPAIR for a pattern that lists categories and would miss the products of one the sample does not show. Check that the pattern matches exactly the URLs labelled PRODUCT and none labelled NOT_PRODUCT; the decision may carry an exclude regex, and a URL is a product URL when it matches value and not exclude. Give NEEDS_REPAIR for a value that leaves non-items out by slug prefixes or character-class alternations (such as everything starting `the-a` or `digi`) when exclude would do, naming the products it would miss: the structural value plus a specific exclude is the accepted shape, and an exclude must take only the non-items it names, never a product the sample or the shop shows. Name in risks only what you could not check; the sample's size needs no caveat. describes_one_product is a hint, never proof. For a NO_PATTERN decision, which says the shop has no product pages, approve only when you read the whole sample and found no page of exactly one product for sale: absence cannot be proven from a sample, so give NEEDS_REPAIR when any page you inspected is one product the author labelled NOT_PRODUCT or left unlabelled, or when the sample shows no sign of the shop's catalogue (an empty shop front, a login wall or a bot challenge is no evidence that the shop has none).
