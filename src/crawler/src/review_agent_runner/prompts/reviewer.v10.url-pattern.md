   - Audit it as the author should have. Group the URLs into route families by structure (namespace, identifier position, locale prefix, suffix, trailing slash, query) before reading slug wording. No URL whose structure marks a listing, category, brand, search, pagination, filter, editorial, support, account or cart page may match, and neither may a gift card, voucher, digital product or service (such as a valuation service) the URL names; a generic slug shape that non-product URLs of the sample share is no distinction.
