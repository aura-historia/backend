## Research notes

- Require current first-party evidence of direct art/antique dealing. Do not onboard ordinary camera, record, currency, watch, fashion, craft-material or modern furniture retailers merely because an item is old or called vintage. Held earlier records require a new direct-scope observation before qualification or enrichment.
- Read all root and nested hosted ledgers before claiming identities; existing ownership takes precedence. Foreign leads/enrichment remain in this frontier for their country/owner.
- Cafe24 hidden sold-out controls and Godomall hidden pickup addresses are not operational facts. Check actual stock variables; require an enabled visit option or explicit collection policy. Visits/payment alone do not establish order pickup.
- A bounded ordinary browser may reveal a public client-rendered catalogue. Access failures do not prove closure; an elapsed hiatus does not prove reopening when a retained catalogue was explicitly for viewing.
- Auction names, auction-purchased provenance and generic membership boilerplate do not establish an auction business. Actual bidding/current auction operations do.
- Same-catalogue aliases preserve source IDs. Distinct catalogues under one legal operator require explicit Party reuse. No inventory, dispatch or collection inheritance follows from a legal address or showroom.
- Model/mould numbers, recording/issue/manufacture dates, design history, image uploads and payout deadlines are distinct. Decode legacy CP949 pages losslessly before interpreting evidence.
- Keep partial destination coverage and unknown rates faithful, including item limits, thresholds and remote surcharges. Country selectors establish no international policy.
- Original logos need visual/media/dimension checks. Rejected candidates and dated rechecks remain in the ledger; raw pages, snapshots and previews stay outside the repository under the KR cache.
