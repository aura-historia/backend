Lessons and enrichment gaps:

- Mainland UK free delivery is not all GB; preserve remote/island/regional exceptions. Omit unsupported rates and item-class generalizations. Unspecified destination areas may be retained as unresolved partial coverage.
- Office, returns and registered addresses establish no showroom, pickup or stock. Locality-only evidence remains locality-only; no coordinates or postal codes guessed.
- Separate stock owners require operator allocation; cross-linked shops with the same verified owner and catalogue may be one operator with multiple premises. Never import generic marketplace logos as dealer brands.
- A branch closure or retirement plan is not business closure. Temporary HTTP401/409 is an access gap; reconcile through public first-party pages, never authentication bypass.
- Advice on external auctions and buying at external auctions are distinct from operating an auction house. Preserve unresolved current auction scope as a deferral.
- Platform facts require actual runtime assets or explicit provider attribution; developer credits and form hosts are insufficient. Retain logo-review revisit dates to avoid repeated failed asset fetches.
- Verify the current legal seller separately from its trading name and any dissolved predecessor. A named seller in current sale terms can resolve a shared family brand; related companies and representatives do not automatically need new Parties.
